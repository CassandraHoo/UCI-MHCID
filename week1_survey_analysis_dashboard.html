Where they're really struggling — selling themselves

Comparison trap

Comparing themselves to peers/senior designers is the #1 imposter syndrome trigger. Shows up across every persona.

Fear of bragging

Multiple respondents explicitly name discomfort with self-promotion as "not genuine" or "arrogant." Especially common in introverts and Career Pivoters.

Not knowing their unique value

"I don't know what makes me different" or "I don't know what to sell" — a direct identity gap, especially acute in Career Seekers.

Translating experience

Pivoters struggle to connect past careers to design credibly. Several describe feeling like a "newbie in the room" despite deep domain expertise.

Speaking up as an introvert

Zero extroverts in this cohort. Every respondent is an introvert or ambivert — and many cite this as a structural barrier to visibility.

Portfolio gaps / polish anxiety

Several worried their work is "too academic" or "not polished enough" — even when they privately feel proud of it.
