Common Themes & Trends

Patterns that cut across all four personas

AI Anxiety is Universal

Nearly every respondent mentioned AI — fear of job displacement, tools changing workflows, losing creativity, and the bar rising for entry-level roles.

Job Market Anxiety

The most common goal across all personas is "get a job." Most feel the market is oversaturated, ghosting is rampant, and the entry-level door is closing.

Storytelling & Communication

Almost everyone wants help communicating their work — to interviewers, engineers, stakeholders, or senior leadership — not learning more design tools.

Toxic Work Experiences

A striking number shared stories of bad managers, being ignored, discriminated against, or having their work stolen. "How to deal with toxic people" was a top topic request. There is a desire to learn about psychological safety.

Imposter Syndrome

Multiple respondents explicitly named it. Several more described it without the label — feeling unqualified, not believing they're a designer, or dreading being "found out."

Accessibility is a Priority

Accessibility ranked among the most-selected topic interests. Several respondents want to specialize in it.

Career Pivots are the Norm

A majority are transitioning — from engineering, healthcare, hospitality, academia, aerospace, or graphic design. Very few arrived in UX through a linear path.

Passion for Human Impact

Across experience levels, what excites people most is designing for real users — solving meaningful problems, building empathy into products, and seeing their work make a difference.
